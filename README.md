<h1>Online Retail Store Analytics Project</h1>

<h2> Project Overview </h2>
This project analyses transactional data from an online retail business to identify key revenue drivers, customer purchasing behavior and retention patterns. The analysis was conducted using Python, SQLite, SQL and Tableau Public to transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights.


The dataset contains over 1,000,000 retail transactions from 2009 to 2011 across multiple countries and products.

<h2> Goal </h2>
The primary goal of this project was to better understand:


1. What drives revenue growth?

2. Which customers contribute the most value?

3. How customer retention impacts revenue?


<h2> Business Questions </h2>

1. Which products, time periods and customer segments contribute the most to revenue?

2. What distinguishes high-value customers from low-value customers?

3. What percentage of customers return after their first purchase and how does this impact revenue?

4. Which products have the highest refund rates?

5. Is revenue concentrated among a small number of products?

<h2> Key Analysis and Insights </h2>

<h3> Revenue Overview </h3>

1. Revenue remained relatively stable throughout the year, with notable peaks occurring in March, September, October and December. Seasonal increases during March and December may reflect holiday-driven purchasing behavior associated with Easter and Christmas periods.

2. Product revenue followed a strong Pareto-like distribution, with the top 20% of products contributing approximately 77% of total revenue. This suggests that the business relies heavily on a relatively small subset of products, creating potential revenue concentration risk if demand for top-performing products declines.

3. Several products consistently ranked among the top revenue generators, including White Hanging Heart T-Light Holder, Regency Cake Stand 3 Tier and Jumbo Bag Red White Spotty. However, sales behavior differed significantly between these products. White Hanging Heart T-Light Holder generated revenue primarily through high sales volume (50,429 units sold), whereas Regency Cake Stand 3 Tier generated comparable revenue despite substantially lower unit sales (10,197 units sold), suggesting a more premium pricing position.


<h3> Refund & Retention Overview </h3>

1. Several products exhibited exceptionally high refund rates, including Tree of Noah Festive Scented Candle (139%), Black Cherry Lights (102%) and Multicolour Feather Curtain (100%). In these cases, refunded quantities exceeded net sold quantities during the analysed period. This may indicate product quality concerns, shipping damage, or mismatches between customer expectations and delivered products.

2. Although recurring customers represented less than half of the total customer base (46%), they generated approximately 80% of total revenue. This highlights the significant importance of customer retention and repeat purchasing behavior as major drivers of long-term revenue growth.


<h3> Customer Analysis </h3>

1. Customer revenue was also highly concentrated, with the top 20% of customers contributing approximately 70% of total revenue. This suggests that a relatively small group of high-value customers has a disproportionate impact on overall business performance.

2. High-value customer segments demonstrated significantly higher average order values, purchase frequency and product diversity compared to lower-value segments. These findings suggest that high-value customers are both more engaged and more loyal, reinforcing the importance of retention-focused business strategies.

3. Lower-value customer segments exhibited comparatively higher refund rates than higher-value segments. This may indicate lower customer satisfaction, weaker purchase intent or less familiarity with the company’s products among one-off or infrequent buyers.


<h2> Business Recommendations </h2>

1. Seasonal revenue peaks during periods such as March and December present opportunities for targeted promotional campaigns and seasonal marketing strategies. Leveraging these high-demand periods through discounts and holiday-focused advertising may help maximise revenue generation and customer engagement.

2. Since revenue is highly concentrated among a relatively small subset of products, the business may benefit from reviewing and diversifying its product portfolio. Heavy dependence on top-performing products creates potential revenue concentration risk if customer demand for those items declines.

3. High-performing products should be strategically promoted through featured sections, personalised recommendations and targeted marketing campaigns. Products with strong sales volume may help attract customer traffic, while premium products may benefit from positioning strategies focused on value and quality.

4. Products with unusually high refund rates should be reassessed for potential issues related to product quality, shipping processes or inaccurate product descriptions. Reducing refund rates may improve both profitability and customer satisfaction.

5. Since recurring customers generate the majority of total revenue, the business should prioritise customer retention initiatives. Loyalty programs, exclusive discounts and targeted email campaigns may help strengthen repeat purchasing behavior and increase long-term customer lifetime value.

6. Given that the top 20% of customers contribute a disproportionately large share of total revenue, the business may benefit from developing tailored retention and engagement strategies specifically for high-value customer segments.
